Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 23, 2026

Gobsmacked LLC develops and provides mobile applications under the Gobsmacked Apps name. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our applications, websites, and related services.

The features and data practices of each Gobsmacked app may differ. The App-Specific Privacy Practices section below explains how this policy applies to individual applications.

Information You Enter

Gobsmacked apps may allow you to enter information to perform calculations, create content, customize an experience, save preferences, or generate results.

Unless an app’s specific disclosure below states otherwise, information entered into a Gobsmacked app is processed locally on your device. Gobsmacked LLC does not receive, collect, store, or have access to that information.

Local Data and Storage

Gobsmacked apps may store information, preferences, settings, or saved work locally on your device.

Unless an app’s specific disclosure states otherwise:

  • Locally stored information is not transmitted to Gobsmacked LLC.
  • Locally stored information is not sold to third parties.
  • You can remove it using the app’s available reset or deletion functions or by deleting the app from your device.
  • Deleting an app generally removes information stored within that app. It may not remove files or reports that you previously exported to another location.

Exported and Shared Content

Some Gobsmacked apps may allow you to create, save, export, print, or share reports, images, documents, calculations, or other content.

Exporting or sharing is initiated by you. Once content is saved or shared, its handling is controlled by the destination, application, service, or person you selected.

You are responsible for reviewing exported content and protecting any personal, financial, or sensitive information it contains before sharing it.

Information We Collect

Our general practice is to minimize data collection. Unless an app-specific disclosure states otherwise, Gobsmacked LLC does not use its apps to collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Account credentials
  • Financial information
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Contacts
  • Photographs or files
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Usage or analytics information
  • Diagnostic information
  • Tracking information

If a Gobsmacked app collects or transmits any of these data types, that app’s specific disclosure will identify the information collected, its purpose, whether it is linked to the user, and whether it is shared with a third party.

  • Optional feedback. Some apps may offer an optional feedback form that sends the responses you choose to provide, along with basic app and device information, to the form provider; no financial information or other private app content is included.

Device Permissions

Certain Gobsmacked apps may request access to device features such as the camera, microphone, photo library, files, location, or notifications.

An app will request permission only when the corresponding feature is used. The reason for the request will be explained when permission is requested.

Granting permission does not necessarily mean Gobsmacked LLC receives the underlying information. When information remains on the device, the relevant app-specific disclosure will say so.

You can manage device permissions through your device’s settings.

Third-Party Services

Gobsmacked apps use Apple’s operating-system, App Store, and device services for distribution and operation. Apple may process information under its own privacy policies and terms.

Some Gobsmacked apps may use external data providers, cloud services, analytics tools, crash-reporting services, or other third-party technology. When applicable, these services and their purposes will be identified in the relevant app-specific disclosure.

Gobsmacked LLC does not authorize third parties to use information collected through its apps for unrelated advertising or tracking unless this practice is expressly disclosed.

Advertising and Tracking

Unless an app-specific disclosure states otherwise, Gobsmacked apps:

  • Do not display third-party advertising
  • Do not use advertising identifiers
  • Do not track users across apps or websites
  • Do not sell personal information

If these practices change for a particular app, this policy and the applicable App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before or when the change is introduced.

Data Sharing and Sale

Gobsmacked LLC does not sell or rent personal information.

Gobsmacked LLC does not share information entered into an app when that information remains entirely on the user’s device.

If a particular app transmits information to Gobsmacked LLC or a service provider, its app-specific disclosure will explain what is transmitted, why it is transmitted, and how it is handled.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored only on your device remains there until you delete it through the app, reset the app’s data, or delete the app.

Exported files remain in the location where you saved or shared them until you or the receiving service deletes them.

If a Gobsmacked app stores information on a server or uses a user account, its app-specific disclosure will explain the applicable retention and deletion procedures.

Purchases

Some Gobsmacked apps may offer in-app purchases through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes payment and transaction information according to Apple’s policies.

Gobsmacked LLC does not directly receive your complete payment-card information from Apple.

The app may receive purchase-status information from Apple when necessary to provide, restore, or verify purchased functionality.

Security

Gobsmacked LLC uses reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the information handled by its apps. However, no device, transmission method, or electronic-storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their devices, accounts, and any content they export or share.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Gobsmacked LLC may update this Privacy Policy as its applications, services, and legal obligations change.

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Material changes affecting a particular app will also be reflected in that app’s applicable privacy disclosures.

App-Specific Privacy Practices

More-House

More-House is an educational home-buying planning application.

Users may enter income, rent, savings, mortgage assumptions, property expenses, tax information, household expenses, and other financial estimates. This information is used to perform calculations and generate results within the app.

More-House is an educational planning tool and does not provide tax, legal, lending, insurance, investment, appraisal, or real-estate advice.

Red~Handed

Red~Handed is an entertainment application that uses the device’s camera to capture test photos and videos that may include a subject’s face. The app processes the location of the face only to add visual effects. Red~Handed does not create or collect faceprints, biometric identifiers, facial-recognition templates, identity profiles, or data used to identify or authenticate a person.

All photos, videos, and face processing remain on the user’s iPhone or iPad. This information is not uploaded to Gobsmacked LLC, stored on developer-controlled servers, used for advertising or tracking, or disclosed to third parties.

Red~Handed retains test photos and videos locally on the user’s device for as long as the user chooses to keep them. Temporary face-location information is used only to position visual effects and is not separately retained.

The user controls the collection and use of face data. Camera access can be denied when requested or revoked at any time in iOS or iPadOS Settings by opening Red~Handed’s permissions and disabling Camera access. Revoking Camera access prevents the app from capturing new test photos or videos.

The user controls deletion of locally stored face data. Individual tests can be deleted from Previous Tests, all locally stored test history, photos, verdict images, and videos can be deleted using Settings > Clear All Data, and all information stored within the app can be removed by deleting the app and its data from the device.

Red~Handed does not share face data with third parties. A photo or video leaves the app only when the user deliberately chooses to save or share it using Apple’s system controls. Content saved or shared at the user’s direction is handled by the destination selected by the user and must be deleted from that destination by the user.

Eye on the Sky

Eye on the Sky is an aircraft identification application.

Flight identification history, observations, and preferences are stored locally on the user’s device. The app does not require accounts or personal information, and Gobsmacked Apps does not maintain a centralized user database. The app retrieves publicly available flight data from third-party aviation providers solely for aircraft identification.

Any future analytics would be limited to anonymous, aggregated usage data used solely to improve app performance and reliability.
