OUR MISSION

Apps that you’d think would exist by now, but don’t.

At Gobsmacked Apps, we believe technology should make everyday life simpler, more understandable, and a little more enjoyable.

We build thoughtfully designed mobile apps that solve real problems, answer meaningful questions, and create moments worth sharing. Whether we’re helping someone make an important financial decision, better understand a complex topic, or simply enjoy a laugh with friends and family, our goal is to deliver practical tools that people genuinely enjoy using.

WHAT GUIDES US

Every app we make does at least one of three things.

01

Simplify Hard Questions

We transform complicated topics into clear, intuitive experiences that anyone can understand. Valuable information shouldn’t require specialized knowledge to be useful.

02

Make Data Usable

Turn publicly available or crowdsourced data into useful or fun tools to highlight the data source and its value to the average person. Data has the greatest value when it’s accessed and used.

03

Bring People Together

We create experiences that encourage conversation, connection, and shared moments—whether they solve a serious problem or simply spark laughter.

“If we can save someone time, reduce uncertainty, help them make a better decision, or create a memorable moment with the people around them, we’ve accomplished our mission.”
