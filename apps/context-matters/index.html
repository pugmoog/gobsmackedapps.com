SAME PERFORMANCE. DIFFERENT STORY.

Read it. Pass it. Act it. Reveal it.

One scene for you. Another for everyone watching.

  1. 01

    Read your scene

    The actor takes the phone and reads their scene in private. Commit it to memory. Your audience gets a different story.

  2. 02

    Pass the phone

    Let the audience read its scene while the actor looks away. Keep both prompts secret until the performance is over.

  3. 03

    Act it out

    Perform your scene without words. The audience watches without guessing or coaching. Laughing is encouraged.

  4. 04

    Compare stories

    Tell each other what you saw. Enjoy the misunderstanding, then choose a new actor and play again.
