ONE TABLE. ONE STORY. EVERYONE’S IN.

Gather. Choose your game. Keep going. Laugh again.

No script required. Just pick up where the last person left off.

  1. 01

    Gather around

    Bring two to six players together and put your phone in the middle of the table. Your improv club is ready.

  2. 02

    Choose your game

    Choose the number of players, your age range, and the game you want to play.

  3. 03

    Keep it going

    Mark your position at the table and start the game. Follow the arrow to build on the story.

  4. 04

    Laugh again

    Name and save your session, then listen back. Share the recording with someone who needs a laugh.

Standard Improv

Build a story together. Keep it going as long as you can.

Pass the Buck

Two chances each to hand the story to someone else.

Mandatory Word

Work your assigned word into your turn to stay in the game.

Outburst

Watch for your word and blurt it out when it appears.
