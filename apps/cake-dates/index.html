Important dates have a habit of hiding in different places - until the chance to celebrate has passed.

Our love of cake compels us to do better. CakeDates brings them together, helping you remember the people and moments that deserve a little attention. Missing a birthday is unfortunate, missing cake is entirely avoidable.

FROM SCATTERED DATES TO A SHARED LIST

Add it. Track it. Share it. Remember it.

A little help remembering. A lot more reasons to connect.

  1. 01

    Add a date

    Hold the microphone and say "John’s birthday is September 5." Or type it in. Review the details, then save.

  2. 02

    Track your dates

    See your important dates together, month by month. Browse September, look ahead to October, and keep the next celebration in sight.

  3. 03

    Share with family

    Invite your family to collaborate on one shared list. Bring your dates together so everyone can add, update and remember the occasions that matter.

  4. 04

    Remember the date

    A timely reminder can turn a date into a celebration. Choose day-of or advance alerts, then send a note to make someone’s day.
