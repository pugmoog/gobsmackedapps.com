THE QUESTIONS BEHIND EVERY FIRST HOME

How much house can I comfortably afford?

A lender may tell you what you can qualify for. More-House helps you explore what the payment may leave after taxes, debts and the full cost of owning—so you can think about comfort, not only approval.

What will my real monthly payment be?

Include property taxes, homeowners insurance, mortgage insurance, HOA costs, maintenance, repairs and other assumptions that can change the true monthly cost of a home.

Is buying better than renting for me?

Compare renter cash flow with the modeled cost of ownership, then examine the wealth impact at 3, 5, 10 and 15 years.

Will buying make me house-poor?

See estimated take-home pay, housing costs, debt-to-income ratios and remaining monthly income together, including room for emergencies and everyday life.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Plan with clearer expectations.

Is More-House a mortgage calculator?

It includes mortgage and home-payment modeling, but goes further by connecting a purchase to household cash flow, renter costs, ownership expenses, debt-to-income estimates and longer-term rent-versus-buy outcomes.

Can More-House tell me how much home I can afford?

More-House helps model affordability by estimating upfront cash, monthly housing costs, debt-to-income ratios and income remaining after modeled expenses. It is an educational planning tool, not a mortgage preapproval or lending decision.

Does it include taxes, insurance and PMI?

Yes. It can include property taxes, homeowners insurance, mortgage insurance, HOA costs, maintenance and repairs. You can change down payments, mortgage rates and other assumptions to compare results.

Is my financial information private?

Your calculator inputs and personal financial information stay on your device. No account is required, and More-House does not include advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics.

Is More-House free?

The app is free to download. New users receive 24 hours of introductory access to Premium features. After that, downloadable PDF reports, Wealth Projection and iPad Scenario Mode can be unlocked with a one-time purchase—not a subscription.
